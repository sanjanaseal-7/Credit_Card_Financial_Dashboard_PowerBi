# Credit_Card_Financial_Dashboard_PowerBi
**Power Bi Dashboard**
# Project Overview:
Designed and developed an end-to-end Power BI reporting solution to analyse credit card customer demographics, revenue performance, and transaction behavior. The solution consists of two linked interactive dashboards - a Customer Report and a Transaction Report,enabling stakeholders to evaluate customer profitability, segment performance and spending patterns across quarters for data-driven business decisions.

# Tools & Skills:

• Power BI | Excel | Postgres SQL | Power Query | Data Cleaning | Data Modelling | Data Visualization | Dashboard Design |

# Key Insights Covered:

• Total Revenue: 55M

• Total Interest Earned: 8M

• Total Income: 576M

• Customer Satisfaction Score (CSS): 3.19

• Total Transaction Amount: 44.5M

• Total Transaction Count: 655.7K

# Business Insights:

• Male customers contribute 54% of total revenue (30M) vs. 45% from female customers, revealing a notable gender-based revenue gap.

• Blue cardholders account for 83% of total revenue (46M of 55M), making them the most critical customer segment to retain.

• Swipe transactions drive 64% of revenue (35M), far outpacing Chip (31%) and Online (5%), showing strong preference for in-person payments.

• Transaction count dropped ~3% from Q3 to Q4 (166.6K → 161.6K) despite stable revenue, indicating a late-year engagement dip.
